## What must be reviewed

The audit inventories every currently publishable article, including unflagged and previously sampled content. Each dossier covers:

- Label, aliases, definitions, summary, concept type, facets, and difficulty when present.
- Every approved citation: claim support, topic mapping, object/role/condition qualifiers, duplication, source attribution, and complete timestamps/playback boundaries.
- Every approved relation and its target/topic and selected source support.
- Every visual window or verified visual claim. A supported visual verdict requires an explicit full-clip inspection record; captions alone do not verify a demonstration.

Exact selected captions and bounded nearby context are separate in the private dossier. A nearby line supports a claim only after its segment ID is included in a reviewed source span. Structural checks run across the whole corpus; boundary flags guide inspection but do not establish semantic correctness.

The baseline on 2026-10-14 contains **379 articles, 1,862 citations, 70 relations, and 35 visual review units**. Including article fields, there were initially 4,544 units. The first repaired article removed one misplaced citation and unsupported difficulty, leaving **4,542 current units**. Review articles in stable concept-ID order. No random sampling, risk cutoff, or omission of unflagged records. Finish every unit in an article before recording it. Leave uncertainty unresolved rather than marking it supported. Visual support additionally requires `watched_full_clip: true` after actually viewing the full window.


Partial coverage, stale fingerprints, duplicate article submissions, invalid verdicts, missing notes, or unsupported visual completion fail before the checkpoint is changed. Completion requires every current unit supported and zero structural errors.

## Repair and recheck

Change reviewed canonical YAML only after exact-source inspection, with original fields/evidence archived and the processor idle. Apply the appropriate action: narrow unsupported wording, include actual supporting caption IDs within valid timing limits, consolidate equivalent moments, remove unsupported facets, or quarantine misplaced/unsupported evidence. Do not silently move a citation to a new topic. Do not upgrade visual status without footage inspection.

Refresh the ledger after repairs. Any changed article, selected/context caption, source metadata, visual-video metadata, or relation target invalidates its old review. Changed/removed records and previous decisions remain in history. Re-review the corrected fingerprint before advancing it to supported. A fully quarantined article leaves the public inventory and is retained as no-longer-public history, rather than disappearing without explanation.

Run the complete idle release gate and affected routes after each repair batch; final release verification is required before resuming processing. New content generated later receives new pending units. No LLM model/profile changes, new extraction, or automatic publishing is part of this mechanism. Audit verdicts are editorial observations, not handcrafted replacement extraction candidates.
